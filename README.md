## Shared Syllabus Baseline

The following expectations are shared across classes unless a class syllabus states otherwise.

### Required Materials

- School uniform, including closed-toed shoes.
- Graphing calculator (TI-83 series or similar; TI-89 series not allowed).
- iPad that is fully charged and connected to Wi-Fi.

### Optional Materials

- Optional note-taking device such as an iPad, tablet, or laptop.

### Exam Schedule

- Tests follow the textbook pacing guide.
- A review day is scheduled before each test.
- There are no other officially set testing days.

### Shared Rules

- Read assigned pages and complete classwork by the stated deadlines.
- Keep organized notes during lessons and discussions.
- Use devices only for approved classroom tasks.
- Keep phones away unless there is an emergency.
- Bring required materials each day and keep devices ready for class.
- Follow all classroom rules and school handbook expectations.
- Field trips require respectful and safe behavior at all times.
- Repeated disruptions result in escalating consequences.
- Serious behavior may be referred to the office immediately.

### Shared Grading

- Work is due by the assigned deadline.
- Late or missing work may be marked missing until submitted.
- Missing work may still be accepted during the grading window.
- Incomplete work may be entered as zero until completed.
- Full or partial credit after late completion depends on effort and circumstances.
- Grades are intended to reflect demonstrated understanding.

### Shared Policies

- Academic honesty is required for all assignments and assessments.
- Cheating, plagiarism, hidden notes, or answer sharing are not allowed.
- Academic dishonesty may result in grade and disciplinary consequences.
- Equivalent substitute assignments may be approved when appropriate.

### Shared Safety

- Follow all classroom and lab safety directions.
- Wear required safety gear when instructed.
- Use tools, devices, and equipment only as directed.
- Report unsafe behavior or conditions immediately.
- Unsafe behavior may result in removal from activities and discipline.
