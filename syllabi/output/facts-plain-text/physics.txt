Physics Science Syllabus

Term

Fall Semester 2026

Course Header

- Grades: 12
- Class Time: Monday-Friday, 11:10 am-12:00 pm
- Textbook: Physics by Raymond A. Serway, Ph.D., and Jerry S. Faughn, Ph.D.

Course Description

This course walks students through core physics concepts using a chapter-based progression from the adopted textbook.

Units

- Module 1 Physics Toolkit: Module 1 A Physics Toolkit.

- Unit 1 Mechanics in One Dimension: Module 2 Representing Motion; Module 3 Accelerated Motion; Module 4 Forces in One Dimension.

- Unit 2 Mechanics in Two Dimensions: Module 5 Displacement and Force in Two Dimensions; Module 6 Motion in Two Dimensions; Module 7 Gravitation; Module 8 Rotational Motion; Module Earth and Space Science - Earth Tectonic Processes.

- Unit 3 Momentum and Energy: Module 9 Momentum and Its Conservation; Module 10 Energy and Its Conservation; Module 11 Thermal Energy; Module 12 States of Matter; Module Earth and Space Science - Earth's Resources; Module Earth and Space Science - Earth Surface Processes.

- Unit 4 Waves and Light: Module 13 Vibrations and Waves; Module 14 Sound; Module 15 Fundamentals of Light; Module 16 Reflection and Refraction; Module 17 Interference and Diffraction; Module Earth and Space Science - Seismic Waves.

- Unit 5 Electricity and Magnetism: Module 18 Electrostatics; Module 19 Electric Current and Circuits; Module 20 Magnetism; Module 21 Electromagnetism.

- Unit 6 Subatomic Physics: Module 22 Quantum Theory and the Atom; Module 23 Solid-State Electronics; Module 24 Nuclear and Particle Physics; Module Earth and Space Science - Stars; Module Earth and Space Science - Cosmology.

Shared Grading

Grading and Assignment Policies

Homework and Classwork

- Work assigned in class is due by the stated deadline.
- Late work may be marked missing until submitted.
- Missing assignments may still be accepted through the grading window defined by the instructor.
- Complete all parts of an assignment before expecting full grading.
- Late, missing, or incomplete work is generally graded after on-time submissions.

Incomplete Assignments

- Incomplete work is entered as zero until completed.
- If work is incomplete due to an honest mistake, full credit may be restored after completion.
- If work is incomplete due to poor time use or effort, partial credit may be applied after completion.

Grading Philosophy

- Grades should reflect demonstrated mastery of course standards.
- Students are responsible for staying current with assignments and assessments.
- When appropriate, equivalent assessment substitutions may be used to better measure mastery.
- In rare cases, specific grades may be replaced, dropped, or adjusted based on documented evidence of understanding.
