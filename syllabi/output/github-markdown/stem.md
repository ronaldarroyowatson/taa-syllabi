## Shared Rules

## Class Rules and Learning Goals

### Expectations and Goals

- Read the pages your teacher assigns each night.
- Finish classwork on time each day.
- Keep notes during lessons and class talks.
- Use devices only when your teacher says to.
- Keep phones away unless there is an emergency.
- Follow class rules and school handbook rules.
- Your teacher may update rules to help everyone learn better.

### Materials Rules

- Bring your required class supplies every day.
- Bring charged devices when your class needs them.
- Follow any extra material rules listed for your class.

### Field Trip Expectations

- Show respectful and safe behavior on field trips.
- If behavior is unsafe, you may be removed from the trip.
- Parent drivers must follow school rules.

### Classroom Discipline

- First time: warning.
- Second time: consequence plus notice to home and office.
- Third time or more: office referral and stronger action.
- Serious behavior can go straight to the office.
- If many students disrupt class, the whole class may get a group consequence.

## Shared Grading

## Grades and Assignment Rules

### Homework and Classwork

- Turn in work by the class deadline.
- Late work can be marked missing until you submit it.
- Missing work may still be accepted during the grading period.
- Finish all parts of your assignment so it can be fully graded.
- Late or missing work is often graded after on-time work.

### Incomplete Assignments

- Incomplete work counts as zero until finished.
- If the missing part was an honest mistake, full credit may be possible.
- If work was incomplete because of poor effort or time use, partial credit may be given.

### Grading Philosophy

- Grades should show what you have learned.
- You are responsible for keeping up with work.
- Sometimes your teacher may use a different but equal assignment to check learning.
- In special cases, grades may be changed to better match your understanding.

## Shared Policies

## Academic and Class Policies

### Academic Honesty

- Do your own work.
- Do not copy, cheat, or use someone else's work as your own.
- Do not use hidden notes or share answers during quizzes and tests.
- Cheating can lead to a zero, a parent call, and office consequences.
- Your teacher may allow a supervised redo in some cases.

### Substitute Assignments

- Some students may get alternate assignments for learning needs.
- Alternate work must cover the same skills and depth.
- Alternate quizzes, tests, or projects must still meet deadlines unless approved.
- If a required group project is missed for a school reason, an equal replacement may be used.

## Safety Rules

- Use tools and devices only as directed.
- Tell the teacher right away about unsafe behavior or problems.
- Unsafe behavior can lead to removal from activities and discipline.
