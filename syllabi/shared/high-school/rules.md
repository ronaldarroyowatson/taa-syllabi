## Classroom Rules and Learning Expectations

### Expectations and Goals

- Read assigned pages each night to prepare for class.
- Complete all in-class work before the daily deadline.
- Keep organized notes during lectures and class discussions.
- Use devices only for approved learning tasks.
- Keep phones put away unless there is an emergency.
- Follow all classroom and school handbook rules.
- The instructor may update class rules when needed to improve learning.

### Course Materials Expectations

- Bring all required class materials each day.
- Keep devices charged and ready for class use when allowed.
- Follow all material-specific requirements listed in the class syllabus.

### Field Trip Expectations

- Field trips require professional behavior and strict rule-following.
- Unsafe or disruptive behavior may result in removal from the trip.
- Parent drivers must follow school requirements and background-check rules.

### Classroom Discipline

- First infraction: verbal warning.
- Second infraction: assigned consequence and parent/office notification.
- Third and later infractions: referral to the office for escalated action.
- Serious offenses may be referred to the office immediately.
- Group disruptions may result in group-based classroom consequences.
