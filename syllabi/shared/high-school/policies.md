## Academic and Classroom Policies

## Academic Honesty

- Cheating, plagiarism, and unauthorized collaboration are not allowed.
- Prohibited actions include copying work, using hidden notes, sharing answers,
  or submitting internet content as original work.
- Academic dishonesty may result in a zero, parent or office notification,
  and additional disciplinary action.
- The instructor may allow a supervised reassessment when appropriate.

## Substitute Assignments

- Alternative assignments may be approved for documented learning needs.
- Alternatives must measure the same standards and require comparable depth.
- Alternative quizzes, tests, or projects must follow the original deadlines
  unless otherwise approved.
- Equivalent alternatives may be provided for specific lab objections or missed
  school-sponsored group work when rescheduling is not possible.
